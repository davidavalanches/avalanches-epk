Bio

Avalanches represents a new wave of instrumental heavy music capable of bridging the gap between post-metal intensity and emotionally driven songwriting.

The Nantes-based trio blends post-rock atmospheres, sludge textures and progressive structures into a cinematic and immersive sound built on contrast: massive riffs, hypnotic crescendos, melodic tension and moments of restrained beauty. With releases like Megalithe and Fragile, the band establishes itself as a compelling addition to the European instrumental and post-metal landscape.

Hailing from Nantes, France, Avalanches is an instrumental trio formed by David Poiraudeau (guitar), Guillaume Pastemps (drums) and Nicolas Bertrand (bass) — three musicians united around a shared vision: creating music where crushing heaviness, emotional tension and melodic songwriting coexist naturally.

The name Avalanches reflects the project's core idea — the contemplation of forces far greater than ourselves, exploring the instability hidden beneath everything we perceive as solid, permanent or indestructible.

Blending post-rock, post-metal, sludge and progressive rock, Avalanches crafts dense, cinematic compositions shifting from hypnotic atmospheres to massive riff-driven climaxes, drawing as much from Russian Circles, The Ocean, Tool and Mastodon as from the atmospheric depth of Deftones, Caspian or Radiohead.

In June 2023, Avalanches released its debut EP Megalithe, introducing the band's sonic identity: immersive, heavy and emotionally charged. With Fragile, the trio pushes this duality further, balancing aggression and vulnerability, tension and release — a rare kind of instrumental music: progressive and powerful, yet deeply human and emotionally resonant.
